## 🚨 Problem Statement  
🔹 Some customers, especially those with dietary restrictions like **diabetes**, struggle to manage their food intake at restaurants.  
🔹 Many restaurants lack **efficient tools** to estimate nutritional values of their dishes.  
🔹 Poor **social media marketing strategies** lead to low customer engagement and revenue loss.  

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution  
✅ **Personalized Meal Recommendations** – Users get meal suggestions based on their calorie goals and preferences.  
✅ **Restaurant Inventory Management** – Helps manage excess food and redirect it to food banks.  
✅ **Nutritionist Integration** – Restaurants can consult nutritionists to create healthier menu options.  

---

## 🚀 Features Developed  

### 🍽 Customize Plate Option  
🔸 Users can create a **customized meal** by selecting preferred ingredients.  

### 🔍 Smart Search Feature  
🔸 Voice-based **smart search** helps users find meals based on keywords.  

### 🎯 Smart Search Filter  
🔸 Extracted keywords are displayed for transparency and refined search results.  

### 🤝 Food Bank Integration  
🔸 Restaurants can **donate surplus food** to minimize waste.  

### 📜 Ingredient Transparency  
🔸 Users can view a **detailed ingredient list** for each dish.  

### 🏢 Restaurant Menu Management  
🔸 Restaurants can **easily add and update** their menu items.
